Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Jemsa Media Group respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use this website or contact us.

Information you provide

When you contact us, you may provide your name, company, email address, telephone number, project requirements, budget and timeline. The website enquiry form prepares an email in your own email application; it does not store the form contents on this website.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare proposals.
  • To provide requested services and communicate about projects.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and improve the website.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers only when necessary to operate our business or deliver requested services. We retain information only for as long as reasonably required.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.
